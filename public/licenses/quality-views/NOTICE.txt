3. Electrode montages (neuroschema 0.6.0, views/montages)
   The standard electrode positions that place sensors on a scalp map by name, one file per
   montage, as neuroschema's generator wrote them from MNE-Python 1.13.2 and from
   EEGLAB's sample_locs (sccn/eeglab at 1f8488b81eae). Each montage's source, the
   digest of its source file and its upstream are listed in the site's source code, in
   src/lib/quality-views/vocabulary/montages/PROVENANCE.json. Modified: positions are scaled
   to meters and rounded to 9 decimal places.

   - GSN-HydroCel-*: Electrical Geodesics, Inc. (EGI) HydroCel Geodesic Sensor Net layouts,

   - biosemi*: BioSemi head cap layouts. BioSemi publishes its cap coordinates with no
